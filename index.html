Why now

Six things a growing
estate now needs.

PopShop was set up as a straightforward ordering site, and that is exactly what it has been. What a 130-restaurant estate asks of it today is a different proposition.

01

Ordering under each store's own account

One shared login was perfectly workable at a handful of sites. At 200 or 300 restaurants, every order needs to identify the restaurant it came from automatically.

02

Approvals that route themselves

Today an order emails us, and we forward it to the area manager. Automating that hand-off keeps everything moving as order volumes climb.

03

A dashboard for area managers

Area managers are kept in the loop by email. Their own live view of every restaurant they look after would give them the whole picture at a glance.

04

A catalogue tailored to each store

A halal restaurant, a drive-thru and a family diner all browse the same list today. Showing each store only what applies to it saves everyone time.

05

Onboarding that keeps up with you

With 3 to 4 restaurants opening every month, adding a store, assigning its area manager and setting its materials should take a couple of minutes.

06

Spend visibility for head office

Reporting on what each restaurant orders and what it spends gives head office the oversight an estate of this size deserves.

A custom portal. Built for Popeyes. Not anyone else.

Who uses it and how

Store staff and managers

Their own login. Their own catalogue.

Each restaurant gets its own secure login. When staff log in, they see a catalogue tailored specifically to their restaurant. A halal store sees halal materials. A drive-thru sees drive-thru materials. They can save items to a wishlist, build a basket, upload artwork, and submit in one go.

Area managers

Everything in one place.

Area managers get notified the moment a store submits an order. They log in to their own dashboard, see all their restaurants, and can approve or decline with a single click. They can also place orders on behalf of stores and see a comparison view of ordering activity.

Head office

The full picture.

See spend by store, spend by area, and view a calendar of upcoming campaign launches. Identify which locations are most or least active. All orders are still invoiced to head office in exactly the same way as now.

Marketindex

A clean production queue.

All approved orders in one organised, filterable queue. Orders with custom artwork come with the file attached. We can update status, add tracking links, and manage the entire product catalogue without any technical work.

Smart catalogues
07

Halal stores

How we will

Core portal live

Spend reporting, campaign calendar, campaign packs, bulk ordering for area managers, the reorder button, spending limit warnings, regional and seasonal targeting, and more.
