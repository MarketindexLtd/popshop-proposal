The honest picture

Six things the current
platform cannot do.

The current PopShop site does the basics. But it was built on an off-the-shelf platform that was not designed for how a multi-site restaurant group actually works.

01

No individual store accounts

Anyone with the login can place an order. When an order comes in we cannot automatically tell which restaurant it is from. As you scale to 200 or 300 restaurants, this becomes a serious problem.

02

Manual email approval process

When a store places an order, the email comes to us at Marketindex. We forward it to the area manager and wait for a reply. With 130 stores and growing, things slip through the cracks.

03

Area managers have no dashboard

There is no way for an area manager to see all the orders from their restaurants in one place. They only find out what has been ordered when we forward them an email.

04

Every store sees the same catalogue

A halal restaurant, a drive-thru, and a family diner all see exactly the same materials. Stores wade through things that do not apply to them.

05

Cannot keep pace with growth

You are opening 3 to 4 new restaurants every month. The current system has no quick way to onboard a new store, assign them to the right area manager, or make sure they see the right materials.

06

No spending visibility for head office

No way to see what each restaurant is ordering or how much is being spent. No reporting, no oversight, no way to set or track budgets across the estate.

What we are proposing

A custom portal. Built for Popeyes. Not anyone else.

Same name, same web address, same catalogue of materials. Built from the ground up to work the way Popeyes works - with the roles, approval flows, and visibility your team actually needs.

Who uses it and how

Store staff and managers

Their own login. Their own catalogue.

Each restaurant gets its own secure login. When staff log in, they see a catalogue tailored specifically to their restaurant. A halal store sees halal materials. A drive-thru sees drive-thru materials. They can save items to a wishlist, build a basket, upload artwork, and submit in one go.

Area managers

Everything in one place.

Area managers get notified the moment a store submits an order. They log in to their own dashboard, see all their restaurants, and can approve or decline with a single click. They can also place orders on behalf of stores and see a comparison view of ordering activity.

Head office

The full picture.

See spend by store, spend by area, and view a calendar of upcoming campaign launches. Identify which locations are most or least active. All orders are still invoiced to head office in exactly the same way as now.

Marketindex

A clean production queue.

All approved orders in one organised, filterable queue. Orders with custom artwork come with the file attached. We can update status, add tracking links, and manage the entire product catalogue without any technical work.
